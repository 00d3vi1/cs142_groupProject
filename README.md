The idea is still a card game but more along the lines of a TCG.

I was thinking of having a pool of [Elemental Cards] that both side draw from and then a personal deck of Action Cards or something. Could generate it into an array that we shuffle at the start of the "match". Special effects could trigger a shuffle. 

Each side take a turn drawing one [Elemental] and one [Action] card. Then each side chooses a combination of actions to "cast". The combination probably could go into a "temporary" String array that we can loop through to determine valid combinations and effects that clears after use. The combination could be confirmed with an [action type] with something like [attack], [guard], or whatever for various effects

[Actions] cards could be items such as health/mana potions or tools. 

Each [player] could have resources like Health and Mana. Mana giving soft limits to how many cards that can combine. Mana could have a base regneration per turn with [Mana Potions] boosting that recovery x amount ofit turns. Health reaching 0 results in win/lose.

I think it could be potentially difficult but I think it will have enough for 4 people to contribute with room for extended creativity.
